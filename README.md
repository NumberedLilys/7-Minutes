  # 7-Minutes

**About the Game** 
  
  7-Mintues is a 2D story-based game developed using Python and Pygame. The game follows Maynne, a university student who goes through what seems like an ordinary day.

  The game explores the theme of relationships, time, missed opportunity and regrets. Throught conversations, player choices , charatcter interactions.

**Story**
  
  The game begins in Maynne's bedroom, where she is getting ready for another busy university day. She has classes to attend, messages to reply to, and people she keeps meaning to spend more time with.

  As Maynne moves through her day, she interacts with different characters, including her mother, her best friend Audra, and her cat Lyra. Players can explore the environment, interact with objects, and make choices that influence certain conversations and story moments.

  However, something strange begins to happen as the countdown approaches zero. Maynne encounters a mysterious figure known as Death.

**Game Play**
 
  - Countdown Timer: A seven minute timer will be displayed at the corner of the screen throughout the game
    
  - Object Interactions: Player will be interacting with objects
    
  - Character Interaction: Player can speak to other NPCs
    
  - Player Choices: Certain decisions can affect the dialouges and characters reactions
    
  - Story Explorations: Player will be traveling though different locations which
    ncludes player house, bus stop, university


**Characters**
 
  Maynne - The main character the university student.
  
  Lyra - Maynne's cat. An important part of her life and one of the relationships explored in the story.
 
  Mom - Maynne's mother, who cares about her and regularly checks in on her.
  
  Audra - Maynne's best friend. She misses spending time with Maynne and wants to reconnect with her.
  
  Death - A mysterious figure who appears when Maynne's time runs out.
  
  General NPCs Background characters who help bring the university environment to life.

**Development**
  
  - Python - the main programming language
  - Pygame - used to create the 2d environment, animations, interactions and character movement
    
**Controls**

  - "A" and "D" or "arrow keys"
    
**Main Features**
 
  - Story-driven gameplay.
  - Character interactions and dialogue.
  - Branching dialogue and player decisions.
  - Countdown timer.
  - Multiple environments to explore.
  - Optional interactions that reveal additional story details.
